Wwise Adaptive Music Demo (Cyberpunk 2077 fan project) | Nov 2026 | https://youtu.be/fZxfOGlBAaE
  • Composed 18 minutes of industrial metal and ambient electronic music in Cubase, then rendered and imported individual instrument stems into Wwise 2025.1 to create an adaptive music system; mixed stems into an organized, clearly-labeled bus system allowing for real-time mixing and sidechaining.
  • Designed State Groups based on Environment (Exterior/Interior), Enemy Awareness (Safe/Alert), Game State (Exploration/Combat), and Intensity (Low/High) and drove state changes using Events posted from REAPER to initiate vertical and horizontal resequencing between music cues; so players hear ambient electronic music during exploration, and hard-hitting industrial music during combat.
  • Implemented transition segments using Bridge containers to blend seamlessly between combat and exploration cues, using lowpass filter and highpass filter events to tame harsh frequencies.
  • Designed RTPCs that automate lowpass filter, distortion effects and bus volumes based on player health, allowing glitchy synthesizer layers to fade in and out as players encounter (and recover from) desperate gameplay scenarios.
